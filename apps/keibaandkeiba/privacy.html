Education-only scope and local progress / 教育専用と端末内進捗

The app provides six lessons, 18 questions, review, ten JRA bet types, and combination practice. It does not provide wagering, predictions, odds, payouts, deposits, accounts, or ticket purchase. Age route, lesson progress, missed-item state, and verified ad-removal cache remain on the device. The operator does not upload learning answers or age selection to its own server.

6レッスン、18問、復習、JRA馬券10種類、組合せ練習を提供します。投票、予想、オッズ、払戻、入出金、口座、馬券購入は提供しません。年齢ルート、学習進捗、間違い状態、確認済み広告除去キャッシュは端末内に保存し、学習回答や年齢選択を運営者サーバーへ送信しません。

Age route / 年齢ルート

The full course and monetization surfaces are restricted to users who self-declare age 20 or older. The under-20 route contains only general and responsible-use information and does not initialize RevenueCat, purchase retrieval, UMP, or AdMob.

全学習コースと収益機能は20歳以上と自己申告したルートに限定します。20歳未満ルートは一般知識と責任ある利用情報だけを表示し、RevenueCat、購入取得、UMP、AdMobを初期化しません。

Purchases / 購入

Apple processes the one-time Remove Ads purchase. StoreKit 2 and RevenueCat retrieve and restore entitlement state. RevenueCat uses an anonymous App User ID and automatic device-identifier collection is disabled. It may process anonymous purchase, entitlement, fraud-prevention, and diagnostic data under its policy; the app requests no account name or email.

広告除去の買い切り購入はAppleが処理します。StoreKit 2とRevenueCatで利用権を取得・復元します。RevenueCatは匿名App User IDを使用し、端末識別子の自動収集を無効にします。同社は匿名購入、利用権、不正防止、診断情報を方針に基づき処理する場合があり、本アプリは氏名・メールを要求しません。

Advertising, consent, and control / 広告・同意・制御

Eligible adults who completed lesson one may receive one Home Google AdMob banner only when entitlement, product availability, UMP consent, production identifiers, and the remote control all permit it. Publisher first-party ID and personalization are disabled; the app does not request ATT or direct IDFA access. Google may nevertheless process IP-derived coarse location, app/developer-bounded or advertising identifiers, advertising, product-interaction, crash, performance, fraud-prevention, and diagnostic data as applicable.

A small HTTPS ad-control document is fetched from this host and fails closed: missing, malformed, expired, oversized, wrong-host, non-HTTPS, or network-failure responses keep ads off. Normal web-request data may be processed by the hosting provider.
