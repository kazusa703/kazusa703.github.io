MitaLog legal & support

MitaLog lets you register song references and discover people with similar tastes and new music. These pages describe music-reference and sharing features in version 2.0.1.

Registrations are stored on our server even before publication. Publishing makes the entire registered list available for other users to view and search. You must be at least 16 and self-declare eligibility in the app; we do not verify age using identity documents.
